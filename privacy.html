Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

PANVENEDUX ("we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit panvenedux.com (the "Website"), how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

This Policy should be read alongside our Cookie Policy, which sets out further detail on the cookies and similar technologies used on the Website.

1. Who We Are

PANVENEDUX is a strategic consultancy. For the purposes of data protection law, PANVENEDUX is the data controller in respect of personal data collected through this Website.

Contact: info@panvenedux.com

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information — name, email address and any message content you submit through our contact form.
  • Usage data — information about how you interact with the Website, including pages visited, time on site, device type, browser, and approximate location, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Booking data — information you provide when scheduling a session via Calendly, including name, email address and any notes submitted at booking.
  • Advertising data — interaction data collected via Meta Pixel for the purpose of measuring the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiry — when you submit the contact form, we use your name and email to reply to your message.
  • To manage bookings — information submitted through Calendly is used to schedule and administer consultation sessions.
  • To understand Website performance — we use Google Analytics to analyse how visitors use the Website and to improve our content and services.
  • To measure advertising effectiveness — Meta Pixel allows us to understand whether our advertising campaigns result in Website visits or enquiries.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Consent — for non-essential cookies and analytics or advertising technologies, where required by applicable law.
  • Legitimate interests — for understanding Website performance and improving our services, where this does not override your rights.
  • Contract — for processing information necessary to deliver a consultation service you have requested.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may process your data on our behalf or independently:

  • Google Analytics (Google LLC) — Website usage analytics. Google may process data in the United States. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Ads (Google LLC) — advertising measurement and conversion tracking.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms, Inc.) — advertising measurement and attribution. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and forwards them to us by email. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Calendly — manages appointment scheduling. See Calendly's Privacy Policy.

6. International Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers operate outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions, as applicable.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy, or as required by law. Contact form enquiries are retained for as long as is necessary to manage the enquiry and any resulting engagement. Analytics data is retained in accordance with our Google Analytics configuration.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • The right to access your personal data.
  • The right to correct inaccurate data.
  • The right to request deletion of your data.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing.
  • The right to data portability.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@panvenedux.com.
