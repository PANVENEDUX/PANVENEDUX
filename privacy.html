Contact: info@panvenedux.com

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Contact information — name, email address and any message content you submit through our contact form.

We use the following third-party services which may process your data on our behalf or independently:

6. International Transfers

Some of our third-party service providers operate outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where personal data is transferred internationally, such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards as required by applicable data protection legislation.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy, or as required by law.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, as well as the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how PANVENEDUX ("PANVENEDUX", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit panvenedux.com (the "Website").

This Cookie Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, which provides further information about how we process and protect personal data.

1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Cookies and similar technologies, including pixels and tags, can enable websites to operate correctly, remember preferences, measure website usage and help website operators understand the effectiveness of their marketing activities.

Some cookies are strictly necessary for the Website to operate. Other technologies, including analytics and advertising technologies, are not strictly necessary and are used only where permitted by applicable law and, where required, after you have provided your consent.

2. Technologies We Use

The Website uses strictly necessary technologies and may use additional technologies according to the consent choices you make.

In particular, PANVENEDUX uses:

  • Google tag and associated Google technologies for measurement, analytics and/or advertising-related purposes, depending on the Google services configured on the Website; and
  • Meta Pixel, provided by Meta Platforms, for advertising measurement, attribution and related marketing purposes.

These technologies may use cookies, pixels, tags, device or browser information and other identifiers to collect information about interactions with the Website.

Non-essential Google and Meta technologies are subject to your consent where required by applicable law.

3. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies and similar technologies are required for essential Website functionality, security and the management of your cookie preferences.

These technologies may be used without your consent where permitted by law because they are necessary to provide functionality or a service expressly requested by you.

Blocking strictly necessary cookies through your browser may prevent parts of the Website from functioning correctly.

4. Analytics and Measurement Technologies

Where you have provided the required consent, Google technologies configured on the Website may collect information relating to your interaction with the Website.

Depending on the Google services configured, this information may include information about pages visited, interactions with Website content, approximate location derived from technical information, device and browser information, referral information and other usage data.

We use this information to understand Website performance and how visitors interact with our content, and to improve our Website and marketing activities.

5. Advertising and Marketing Technologies

Meta Pixel

We use Meta Pixel, a technology provided by Meta Platforms, to help us understand and measure the effectiveness of our advertising activities.

Subject to your consent where required, Meta Pixel may collect information about your browser or device and your interactions with the Website. This may allow us and Meta to measure actions taken after interacting with advertising and evaluate campaign performance.

Depending on your relationship with Meta and your settings on Meta services, Meta may process information collected through these technologies in accordance with its own privacy terms.

Google Advertising Technologies

Where advertising functionality is enabled through the Google services configured on the Website, Google technologies may be used to measure advertising performance, understand conversions and support advertising-related activities.

Such non-essential advertising technologies will only be activated where permitted by applicable law and, where consent is required, after you have provided that consent.

Google processes information collected through its technologies in accordance with its own applicable privacy terms.

6. Your Consent

When you visit the Website, you may be presented with a cookie consent banner or preference mechanism.

Non-essential cookies, pixels and tags, including advertising and analytics technologies, will only be activated where permitted by applicable law and, where required, after you have provided your consent.

You may refuse non-essential technologies without losing access to the core functionality of the Website.

Your decision not to consent must be respected in the same way as a decision to consent.

7. Changing or Withdrawing Your Consent

You may withdraw or change your consent at any time through the cookie preference mechanism available on the Website.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Browser controls are separate from the consent controls provided by the Website.

8. Third-Party Providers

Certain cookies and similar technologies used on the Website are provided by third parties. These include, where applicable:

Google

Google technologies may be used for Website measurement, analytics and advertising-related purposes. Google may receive information generated through its technologies and process that information in accordance with its own privacy and data protection terms.

Meta Platforms

Meta Pixel is provided by Meta Platforms and is used for advertising measurement and related marketing purposes. Meta may receive and process information generated through Meta Pixel in accordance with its own privacy terms.

The processing undertaken independently by these third parties is also governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

9. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on the technology used and your consent choices, cookies, pixels and tags may process information such as:

  • device and browser information;
  • IP address and related technical information;
  • pages visited and Website interactions;
  • referring website or source;
  • date and time of interactions;
  • advertising and campaign interaction information;
  • cookie or device identifiers; and
  • conversion or measurement information.

We do not use these technologies with the intention of collecting special categories of personal data through the Website.

10. Cookie Duration

Cookies may be either session cookies, which generally expire when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a defined period unless deleted earlier.

The precise duration depends on the particular cookie, its purpose and the third-party service that provides it.

Where available, information about individual cookies, their providers, purposes and duration may be displayed through the Website's cookie preference mechanism.

Where personal data is transferred internationally, such transfers are subject to the safeguards and transfer mechanisms required by applicable data protection legislation.

For further information regarding transfers undertaken by Google or Meta, please consult the privacy information provided by the relevant provider.

Where information collected through cookies or similar technologies constitutes personal data, you may have rights under applicable data protection law, including the GDPR.

Depending on the circumstances, these may include the right to request access to your personal data, correction or deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and the right to object to certain processing.

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority.

Further information about your rights and how PANVENEDUX processes personal data is available in our Privacy Policy.
